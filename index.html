About AURUM Protocol

Revolutionizing Stablecoins with Gold

AURUM Protocol introduces a novel approach to stablecoins by algorithmically pegging its value to gold, the world's oldest and most trusted store of value.

Unlike traditional Fiat-backed tokens that require 1:1 physical reserves, our algorithmic model uses sophisticated economic mechanisms to maintain price stability while maximizing capital efficiency.

Combining the timeless value of gold with cutting-edge blockchain technology, we're creating a stable digital asset that bridges traditional finance and the crypto economy.

VALUE MEETS BLOCKCHAIN

How It Works

Peg Mechanism

Continuously monitors the gold price through decentralized oracles and adjusts token supply to maintain the peg.

Expansion & Contraction

When AUR trades above the gold price, new tokens are minted. When below, tokens are burned to restore equilibrium.

Reserve Backing

A portion of protocol revenue is used to build a growing gold reserve, providing additional stability to the system.
